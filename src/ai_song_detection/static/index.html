    A / THE METHOD

    Patterns, not guesswork.

    The detector looks for frequency artifacts that audio generators can leave behind. It analyzes the first 300 seconds of a track.

    B / THE COVERAGE

    Know its range.

    The upstream model targets Suno ≤ 5 and Udio ≤ 1.5. New generators, mixed production, and modified audio can affect results.

    C / THE EVIDENCE

    Keep the numbers in context.

    The upstream project reports 99.88% accuracy on its own held-out test set. We have not independently verified that result.
